# Verification debt in AI-assisted review (fictional test manuscript)

## Introduction

It is well established that AI assistance speeds up peer review (Alvarez, 2021). Several studies show that
reviewers accept model suggestions without checking them (Berg & Ncube, 2020; Berg et al., 2022; Chen, 2019).
Reviewer fatigue causes a 37% drop in error detection (Okafor et al., 2018).

## Evidence

Fabricated sources are common in model output (Dimitrov, 2023). Early work on citation drift
(Hughes, 2017) is often cited, and preprint evidence suggests larger effects (Ivanova, 2022).
Moreover, a structural account of review incentives (Jansen, 2016) remains influential, and
further claims rest on unpublished observations (Nobody, 2015).
